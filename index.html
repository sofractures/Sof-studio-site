— THE STUDIO

sound of fractures

jamie reddington (sound of fractures) is a uk-based artist and researcher working at the intersection of music, memory, and participation. most artists release content. this studio builds containers for meaning.

container for meaning (n.) — a song, an event, a book, an archive: anything built so that other people's lives can live inside it, not just pass by it.

the studio exists to create: experiments in human connection and participation, with music as the seed. it researches by making. it creates knowledge and shares it. it doesn't offer answers — each experiment grows out of the last.

underneath it is a bigger question: what a creative career looks like when a few companies own the rails. music went first — and every medium is now on the same tracks. the research into that future is currently run by the platforms. the studio runs it from the other side — led by an artist, with the audience in it, shared as it happens.

THE CONTAINER

the ethos is the fixed point — connection, participation, process shared. everything else moves with its moment: the medium, the technology, the shape each experiment takes. projects end; the thread doesn't. the studio is the container for all of it.

THE TRAP

"being easy to understand gets good clicks. the dj, the pads producer, the modular head — ready-made lanes, someone else's rules. i didn't want to choose one. so the studio builds the alternative in public: the use case, so people can see it, hear it, take part, and want it to exist."

— jamie reddington
THE METHOD

artist → experiment → community → archive → next experiment.

the output is never the endpoint — each artifact becomes evidence for the next exploration.

PROOF OF WORK
scenesan album that became participatory art — a decentralised, artist-owned platform. a song became a prompt, the prompt gathered strangers' memories, the memories became artefacts, the artefacts became an exhibition, a book, a peer-reviewed chapter, a case study taught on stages from amsterdam to hamburg — designed to remind people of the emotional value of music
alone togetherpart immersive exhibition, part live show, part community event — an ep and an experiment testing whether you can design architecture for genuine connection: alone, then together, then alone together — instead of putting strangers in proximity and hoping
network archivesthe method turned outward — edition 001: a directory of inspiration gathering 60 worldbuilding artist case studies, curated by me with metalabel; 120 digital editions collected
